# Security policy

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately. If **Report a vulnerability** appears on the repository's [Security Advisories page](https://github.com/ArdaKoksall/cosign-junction/security/advisories), use it to send a private report. Otherwise, [open an issue](https://github.com/ArdaKoksall/cosign-junction/issues/new) asking [@ArdaKoksall](https://github.com/ArdaKoksall) for a private reporting channel. Keep the public issue to that request.

In your private report, include the affected version or commit, the affected component, and the potential impact. Remove credentials and buyer or supplier records from any logs or screenshots you share.
